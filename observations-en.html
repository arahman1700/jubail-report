1

The fence and mesh are weak; they were cut and entered through in a short time

Very high risk
  • Temporary fence: sheet-metal panels on mesh, and unreinforced concrete bases (purchase order 15627).
  • Report of 25 October: the fence is leaning heavily outwards, and its post bases are out of the ground.
  • 16 November: bracing and fixing of the fence were not completed (Logistics email).
  • The thieves entered through an opening at the corner under the floodlight, where the mesh appears cut, and a wide opening was made for the vehicle to enter.
  • The whole theft took about 30 minutes. The place and moment of entry do not appear in the recording.

Action taken

The wide opening was closed on 21 November (repair email 12:04). Temporary concrete barriers were moved from Wadi Al-Dhahran and lined up in front of the fence from the inside on 23 November: 140 barriers on site, and 170 stated in work order 82.

Required solution

  • Immediate: Inspect the corner opening (the thieves' entry), close it and reinforce it, and confirm that the 21 November closure covered it (S5).
  • Urgent: Fix the fence bases to an engineer's design, supports for all sections, fix the bottom edge of the mesh to the ground, and a handover record and quality inspection (S18, S1).
  • Decision: A permanent perimeter barrier 920 m long to a single specification; the current quotes are not comparable (S19). A low-cost option: an earth berm outside the fence using the existing loader (S46).
