Surveillance: cameras, internet and electricity

3–5

Cameras, internet, power and lighting

Internet connectivity at the site is weak, and there are no nearby telecommunications towers. A stable connection is needed to link the warehouse cameras to the main monitoring centre.

Camera and UPS photos cropped from works by JensPersson and AnthDaniel, licensed CC BY-SA 3.0.

A quotation was obtained from an additional diesel supplier to support continuity of fuel supply.

Actions required

  • Assess alternatives and set up reliable internet service at the site.
  • Complete installation of all planned cameras and connect them to the main monitoring centre and the monitoring app.
  • Provide backup power using uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) and improve site lighting.

Guarding

6

Guarding and shift handover

The review identified a need to formalise shift handovers and document incident response.

Action taken

Storage and locks

7–8

Reinforce storage containers and install stronger locks

Containers used for high-value materials need stronger locking hardware and reinforcement to protect their contents.

Action taken

The available photos show temporary barriers placed in front of the containers.

Required solution

  • Procure suitable high-security container locks, then install and inspect them.
  • Reinforce the container doors and structure, and secure air-conditioning and ventilation openings while preserving their function.

9

No documented action so far.

Required solution

  • Urgent for decision: a written engineering decision on the level and drainage (S37).
  • Urgent: settle the gravel contractor's dues or assign a replacement to complete the spreading, as a separate item (S41).
