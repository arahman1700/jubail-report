What made it easier

  • Very highA temporary fence leaning outward, its bases failed, and its bracing incomplete four days before the incident. →
  • Very high50 cameras installed out of 125: recording only, no alarm, and no evidence of live monitoring. →
  • Very highNo internet despite the request since at least 13 October: no remote viewing and no alerts. →
  • Very highIn-house guarding with no security company, no vehicle patrol and no shift handover log; about 59 minutes with no visible guard that morning. →
  • HighTwo temporary gates with no contracted locks and no gate log, at an isolated desert site on a Friday morning. →

Why we got here

  • Very high“Minimum” because the requirement exceeded the budget: of 9 items requested by Logistics, only the panel cladding was added. →
  • Very highThe yard was opened for receiving on 21 October before it was ready, and receiving continued after the “not suitable” warning on 11 November. →
  • Very highThree orders for 3,001,136 SAR excl. VAT calc.; the largest, 15609, signed by the contractor alone. →
  • Very highThe two closed warehouses (order 15609), one of them for copper, not completed as of 22–23 November. →
  • HighLate payments: the fence contractor had no payment until 9 November, and diesel has no purchase order, which its suppliers require. →
  • HighNo single named owner for yard security, and the preliminary security survey plan (29 July) was put on hold without update. →
