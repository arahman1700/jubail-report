Why the theft happened

How the theft happened

  • Two masked persons entered through the corner opening of the fence; first appearance inside it at 09:43:24. →
  • A vehicle entered at 10:03:06 through the wide opening, and left loaded after 10 minutes and 28 seconds. →
  • The lock of the blue container was broken: a two-handled tool at its corner at 09:52, and the door leaf was opened 29 seconds after the vehicle entered. →
  • 30 minutes and 30 seconds with no intervention and no visible patrol; first visible check at 10:16, after the vehicle left. →
  • Camera CH21 turned toward the sky at 09:59, before the vehicle entered, with no visible reaction. →

What made it easier

  • A temporary fence leaning outward, its bases failed, and its bracing incomplete four days before the incident. →
  • 50 cameras installed out of 125: recording only, no alarm, and no evidence of live monitoring. →
  • The network is weak and there are no towers in the area, and internet has been requested since 15 October: no remote viewing and no alerts. →
  • In-house guarding with no security company, no vehicle patrol and no shift handover log; about 59 minutes with no visible guard that morning. →
  • Two temporary gates with no contracted locks and no gate log, at an isolated desert site on a Friday morning. →

Why we got here

  • “Minimum” because the requirement exceeded the budget: of 9 items requested by Logistics, only the panel cladding was added. →
  • The yard was opened for receiving on 21 October before it was ready, and receiving continued after the “not suitable” warning on 11 November. →
  • Three orders for 3,001,136 SAR excl. VAT calc.; the largest, 15609, signed by the contractor alone. →
  • The two closed warehouses (order 15609), one of them for copper, not completed as of 22–23 November. →
  • Late payments: the fence contractor had no payment until 9 November, and diesel has no purchase order, which its suppliers require. →
  • No single named owner for yard security, and the preliminary security survey plan (29 July) was put on hold without update. →
